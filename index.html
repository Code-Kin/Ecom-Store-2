From Sarah

A note from the woman whose name is on the door.

A small room, forty pieces, a pot of rooibos.

I opened Sarah's in 2018 above a coffee shop on Kloof Street. It is still one room, one rail, one window — and a small workroom at the back where alterations happen while you wait.

Every piece is made inside South Africa, in batches of forty or fewer. We buy cloth before we draw a pattern.

"Nothing here is seasonal. If it sold out last winter, it is on the roll again this one."
— Sarah Founder · Cape Town
