From Sarah

A little about Sarah, the shop, and the people who make each piece.

A neighbourhood shop, a thoughtful collection, and a pot of rooibos.

I opened Sarah's in 2018 on Kloof Street. Since then, it has stayed true to its beginnings: a welcoming space, a carefully chosen rail, and a small workroom where we can help with alterations.

We work with South African makers who produce in small batches. We start with the cloth, then make pieces designed to be worn well and kept for a long time.

"We choose each piece for how it feels to wear, how it is made, and how beautifully it will live in your wardrobe."
— Sarah Founder · Cape Town
